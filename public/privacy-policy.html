Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Flujo - Cash Flow Control ("the Application", "we", "our", or "us") handles user information.

1. Information We Collect

Flujo is designed with a strong privacy-first approach. The Application does not collect, store, transmit, or share personal or financial information on external servers.

All financial data entered into the Application, including but not limited to:

  • Income and expenses
  • Budgets
  • Credit card information
  • Cash flow records
  • Account balances

is stored exclusively on the user's device using local device storage.

2. How We Use Information

The information entered into the Application is used solely to provide the core functionality of personal financial tracking and visualization within the app itself.

Because the Application does not use cloud servers or remote databases, we do not have access to your financial or personal data.

3. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share user data with third parties.

The Application does not include:

  • Advertising SDKs
  • Behavioral tracking tools
  • Third-party analytics services that collect personal data
  • Data brokers or marketing platforms

4. Permissions

The Application may request limited device permissions only when necessary for app functionality, such as importing or exporting local backup files.

These permissions are used exclusively on-device and are never used to collect or transmit personal information.

5. Data Security

Since all data remains locally stored on the user's device, users are responsible for maintaining the security of their device, including screen locks, backups, and device encryption if desired.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Users have full control over their data at all times.

All stored information can be permanently deleted by:

  • Uninstalling the Application
  • Clearing the app storage or cache through Android system settings

7. Children's Privacy

The Application is not directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in functionality, legal requirements, or platform policies.

Any future feature involving internet connectivity, cloud synchronization, or external services will require an updated Privacy Policy and, where applicable, explicit user consent.
